VocabGo ES — Support

VocabGo ES is a Spanish vocabulary app for learners from A1 to C2 — search, quizzes, flashcards, and a spaced-repetition system that adapts to you.

Contact

For bug reports, feature requests, or general questions, email contact@piraeus.app.

We aim to respond within a few business days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does VocabGo ES require an internet connection?

No. The app works completely offline once installed. Internet is only needed for downloading the app and for processing optional Tip Jar purchases.

Do I need an account?

No. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no personal data collection. Your progress is stored locally on your device.

Will my progress sync across devices?

Not currently. Each device keeps its own progress. Cross-device sync may be added in a future version.

How do I reset my progress?

You can clear your search history from the Search tab, and full data reset is available by uninstalling and reinstalling the app.

What is the Tip Jar?

An optional way to support development. Tips do not unlock any features — the app remains fully free with no ads regardless of whether you tip.

How do I report a missing or incorrect translation?

Use the Send Feedback option in the More tab, or email us directly. Please include the Spanish word and the issue.
